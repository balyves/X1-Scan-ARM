# Scan

The Scan tab (⌥⌘2) sets the resolution and the scale, and shows the sizes.

## Resolution {#resolution}

- The scan resolution, in pixels per inch (ppi).
- It is saved. For now the scan stays at 2 000 ppi.

## Scale {#scale}

- The size of the output relative to the input, in percent.
- It is saved. It will be applied in a later step; until then the Output size does not include it.

## Input and Output {#sizes}

- Input: the pixels of the image under the frame, or of the whole image.
- Output: the size of the file an export would write, in square pixels (see [Export](output.md#export)).

## Scan {#scan-button}

- The “Scan” button is at the bottom of the panel, next to “Preview”. Also: Scanner › Scan… (⇧⌘R).
- An [export folder](output.md#folder) is needed first.
- Today the scan replays FlexColor’s 2 000 ppi scan, with its frame. The panel’s frame does not change the area scanned, and is not applied to the scan received: see [Framing](prepare.md#framing).
- “Stop” (⌘. or Esc) stops it.
- At the end, the 16-bit TIFF file is saved in the export folder, with its recipe.
