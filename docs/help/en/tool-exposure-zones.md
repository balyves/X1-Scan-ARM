# {icon:exposure-zones} Exposure Zones

The exposure zones show on the preview the pixels to watch.

- Button {icon:exposure-zones} of the tool column: show or hide the zones.
- Red: clipped pixels, too bright for the sensor.
- Violet: pixels near the noise, almost black for the sensor.
- The zones come from the last complete preview. See [Exposure](information.md#exposure).
- They are never exported.
