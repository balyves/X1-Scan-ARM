# Prepare: Original and Framing

The Prepare tab (⌥⌘1) describes the original and the framing of the image.

## Original {#original}

### Film {#film}

- Choose the type of film: Positive, Colour negative or B&W negative.
- The choice is saved. It will be applied to the scanner with real scans, later.

### Holder {#holder}

- Choose the holder that is in the scanner.
- Only the holders ticked in Settings › Holders appear.
- The button on the right opens Settings › Holders. You can add a custom holder there.

## Framing {#framing}

Framing changes the display and the export. It never changes what the scanner scans.

### Orientation {#orientation}

- The small frame on the left shows the state: turned or not, mirrored or not.
- Rotate 90° Counterclockwise (⌘[), Rotate 90° Clockwise (⌘]) and Flip Horizontal.
- The same commands are in the Image menu.
- The frame and the angle follow the image.

### Proportions {#proportions}

- Free, Original (those of the image), common formats, or Custom….
- The button next to it swaps width and height (key X while cropping).
- The lock keeps the proportions: the frame keeps its shape while it is resized.

### Angle {#angle}

- Fine rotation of the image, in degrees, up to ±45°.
- With the Crop tool or the Rule, the ← → arrows go to the next whole degree. With Shift, to the next multiple of 5°.

### Straighten {#straighten}

- “Rule” chooses the [Straightening Rule](tool-rule.md).
- “Auto (Edges)” will come in a later version.

### Frame grid {#crop-grid}

- The grid shown inside the frame while cropping: None, Thirds, Fine Grid or Centre Cross.
- “Tint” sets the colour and opacity of the grid lines, as the [Grid](tool-grid.md) menu does. Display only.

### Constrain to image {#constrain}

- Keeps the frame entirely inside the turned image, with no empty corners.

### Reset Framing {#reset}

- The button on the Framing title row puts the orientation, proportions, angle and frame back to the original.
- Edit › Undo (⌘Z) cancels it, as long as nothing else has changed.

### Done {#done}

- While cropping, “Done” (Return) keeps the changes and goes back to the previous tool.
- Esc cancels the changes made since cropping started.
