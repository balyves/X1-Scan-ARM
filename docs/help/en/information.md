# Information: Histogram and Exposure

The Information area is at the bottom of the panel, in every tab.

## Histogram {#histogram}

- The levels of red, green and blue in the image shown.
- With a frame, only the pixels inside it count.
- “Logarithmic” shows small counts next to large peaks.

## Exposure {#exposure}

- Measured after each preview, on the sensor’s raw values.
- “CCD range used”: the part of the sensor’s range the image takes.
- “clipped”: the part of the pixels too bright for the sensor.
- The “Show clipped and noise zones” box shows these pixels on the image: see [Exposure Zones](tool-exposure-zones.md).
- Measuring changes nothing in the image.
